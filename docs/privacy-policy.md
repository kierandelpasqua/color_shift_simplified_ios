# KDP Color Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 25, 2026_

KDP Color (the "Color Shift" app for iPhone) **does not collect any data.**

- **No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.** The app contains no third-party code.
- **No network requests.** The photos and fonts are part of the app. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere.
- **On your device only:** the app remembers one setting, whether haptic feedback is on. It is stored on
  your iPhone and removed when you delete the app.
- **When you share.** Copy link and Export put a link or a Markdown file on your clipboard or into the share
  sheet. Where it goes next is up to you and the app you choose, and that app's own privacy policy applies.
- **Links you open.** Photo credits open the photographer's page on Unsplash in your browser. Unsplash's
  privacy policy applies there.
- **Crash reports.** If you have chosen to share analytics with Apple, Apple may send the developer anonymous
  crash reports. This is controlled in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to access, export or delete.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
